| --- | --- |
| Source ingestion, immutable snapshots and comparisons | [Provenance](../../docs/source-provenance.md), [source comparison](../../docs/source-comparison.md), [source changes](../../docs/source-changes.md) |
| Synthesis, investigation, bridges and applied experience | [Synthesis](../knowledge-synthesis.md), [investigation](../knowledge-investigation.md), [bridges](../research-bridges.md), [applications](../knowledge-applications.md) |

These workflows do not automatically spawn models, invent participants, grant
consensus, approve Work or promote fiction/application failure into fact.
Community posts, comments, rooms, workshops, roleplay, quests and creative
sessions were removed. Legacy discussion files remain read-only history.

Example: Record a long analysis as an ordinary note with exact references.
