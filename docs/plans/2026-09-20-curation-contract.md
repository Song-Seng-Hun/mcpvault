# Contract

related: material association. close_match: similar, not automatically mergeable.
same_as: conceptual identity, not permission to combine documents.
contrasts_with: mutual contrast on a common axis; not factual contradiction.
contradicts: a challenged claim; never silently convert contrast to contradiction.

## Preservation

Keep conditions, exceptions, names, quantities, evidence and source families.
Merge into an explicit canonical target or a new managed target; retain old paths.
Split one input into 2-4 chapters; retain the original path as an outline.
Keep exact historical revisions and anchors; unresolved compatibility blocks apply.
Keep tasks/claims/logical counts independent from physical chapter counts.
Prepare outputs before redirecting originals; use <=10 existing-file change sets.
Persist intent and individual receipts. Reread uncertain writes before retry.
Only revert matching applied revisions; concurrent manual edits require review.

## Retention and graph

180-day age is a review cue, never proof of disuse.
Usage decisions distinguish >=90 days of observed coverage from unknown history.
Auto-archive only receipt-managed duplicates/replacements with verified coverage.
Protect warnings, counterexamples, active work, unique evidence and legal holds.
Human confirmation pins exact deletion targets, hashes and backup scope.
Shared/unremovable backups prevent a complete-deletion claim.
Auto-clean only owned disposable rows/files with verified absence of references.
Preserve authored direct relations despite alternate paths; never auto-break cycles.
At most 200 inspected relations/request; incomplete closure blocks apply.
Hidden references yield no titles, counts or existence details.

## Acceptance

Fixed 60 cases before implementation; no candidate/validation source-family overlap.
Test drift, response loss, restart, cancellation, NAS loss, anchors and scope leaks.
Mandatory safety/meaning regressions pass; Recall@5 and MRR do not decrease.
Measure new 100k/1M candidate/edge paths; do not reuse old memory benchmark claims.
Separate synthetic correctness, live service, next-use benefit and model cost.
