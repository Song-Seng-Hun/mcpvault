- Automatic execution configuration, account bindings and source protection unchanged.
- Positive body-result facts are account-separated and revision-aware, not use evidence.
- Raw source text, paths and credentials are absent from persisted delivery facts.
- The canonical completion record precedes disposable observation indexing.

- Missing/intermittent observations never prove 90 days of disuse or authorize retirement.
